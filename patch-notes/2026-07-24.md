# 2026-07-24

## Added
- Failed downloads now show *why* they failed, at every stage of the Spotify → Deezer pipeline: fetching from Spotify (bad link, expired session, rate limit, malformed response), matching a track to Deezer (not found, or a real Deezer-side failure), and downloading the audio itself (expired token, streaming failure).
- A whole playlist/track failure now pops up a dialog and marks the row red, instead of leaving it stuck on "Preparing tracks…" forever with no feedback.
- Per-track error rows show the actual failure reason inline (e.g. "Song - Artist - Album ⚠ The track could not be found on Deezer.") instead of a bare red row.
- The Spotify connection status in the title bar updates the instant you paste a new link, instead of only after a download fails.
- "Connect Spotify" now actually reconnects — it clears any cached session first, so a stale or mismatched token can't be silently reused. Previously, reconnecting was a no-op if the cached token was already broken.

## Fixed
- A track that failed Spotify→Deezer conversion for an unexpected reason could crash that download job instead of being reported as an error.
- Error rows for failed conversions could vanish entirely after switching screens.
- An unexpected failure downloading a single track (e.g. fetching cover art) could silently kill deeRip's entire background job queue, requiring an app restart.
- Deezer-side conversion failures (timeouts, malformed responses) were mislabeled as "track not found on Deezer" even when the track genuinely existed.
